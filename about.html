About Us

“Quality food, affordable prices, and a family tradition passed down through generations.”

Cherry-Andrew’s Catering Services is a family-owned catering business founded in 2015 by the owner’s mother. The business was named by the owner’s parents, Cherry and Andrew, and was inspired by their grandmother from Corner Paso, Santa Rosa, who passed down the family’s passion for cooking and serving delicious food. Our business continues to carry this family tradition by providing quality food and catering services at affordable prices. We believe that good food and excellent service should be accessible to customers regardless of their budget. Our Commitment At Cherry-Andrew’s Catering Services, we are committed to continuing our family’s legacy, providing quality food at reasonable prices, and ensuring that every client receives dependable and satisfying catering service.
